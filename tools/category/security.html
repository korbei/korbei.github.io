DevTools/Security & privacy

Category · 9 tools

🔐Security & privacy

Inspect the security-sensitive parts of an app locally, with nothing uploaded: decode and verify JWTs or sign new ones, read X.509 certificates, compute hashes and HMACs, and check images and documents for hidden metadata before you share them.

🌳

ASN.1 Decoder

Decode ASN.1 DER/BER — paste PEM, Base64, or hex and see the tag–length–value tree behind X.509 certificates, keys, and CMS, entirely in your browser.

📜

Certificate Decoder

Decode X.509 / PEM certificates — view subject, issuer, validity, SANs, key, and fingerprints, all in your browser.

◇ works well with JWT Debugger

#️⃣

Hash Generator

Compute MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384, and SHA-512 hashes of text or a file, plus HMAC — all at once, entirely in your browser.

◇ works well with Encoder / Decoder

🔐

JWT Debugger

Decode and inspect JSON Web Tokens: view the header and payload, check expiry, and verify HS/RS/PS/ES signatures — all in your browser.

◇ works well with JWT Signer

🔏

JWT Signer

Build a JWT header and payload and sign it with HMAC (HS256/384/512) or an RSA/ECDSA private key (RS/PS/ES) — entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

◇ works well with JWT Debugger

🕵️

Metadata Inspector

Inspect the hidden metadata in a photo or document — EXIF, GPS location, author, dates, and more — right in your browser. Download a cleaned copy with the metadata removed.

◇ works well with Metadata Stripper

🧼

Metadata Stripper

Strip EXIF, GPS, author, and hidden metadata from images and documents in one batch — locally in your browser. JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, PDF, Office, OpenDocument & EPUB.

◇ works well with Metadata Inspector

🔒

Password Hash Generator

Hash a password with Argon2id, bcrypt, scrypt, or PBKDF2 — with OWASP-recommended parameters — and verify a password against a hash, entirely in your browser.

🖍️

PDF Redactor

Redact a PDF so the text is permanently removed, not just covered — and strip its hidden metadata, comments, and attachments. Entirely in your browser, nothing uploaded.
